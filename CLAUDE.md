# CLAUDE.md

## Read first

**[BUSINESS-DOCTRINE.md](./BUSINESS-DOCTRINE.md)** is the canonical
source of truth for positioning, product decisions, onboarding, and
creator acquisition. Read it before making product, UI, or data-model
decisions here — in particular §6 (recommendations are bigger than
products), §11–13 (onboarding from existing content, and not
pre-building every question), §20 (the creator's expertise is the source
of truth — never invent a recommendation), and §21 (UI philosophy: the
card makes the recommendation actionable, the agent's reply carries the
explanation).

One-sentence summary (the doctrine has the full version): we are building
for the person people go to when they want to get better at something — an
authority whose audience trusts their judgment — and making that person's
expertise and recommendations directly retrievable by AI agents when their
audience needs them.

## What this repo is

This is the actual application ("Creator Picks") — see `README.md` for
the technical shape (MCP server, matcher, admin dashboard, demo page).
`BUSINESS-DOCTRINE.md` is the why; `README.md` is the how. This repo is
ground truth for what the product actually does: `bakariw98-tech/landing-page`
(the marketing site) should be kept in sync with what ships here, not the
other way around.
